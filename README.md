# Bitcoin Cycle Bottom Research — June 30, 2026

## Investment Research & Power BI Dashboard

This project investigates whether Bitcoin's **June 30, 2026 low** represents a credible cycle-bottom candidate and translates the evidence into an investment decision framework.

The research combines **market structure, on-chain valuation, capital flows, derivatives positioning, and historical Bitcoin cycle analysis** in a Power BI research environment.

---

## Research Question

> **Does the evidence surrounding Bitcoin's June 30, 2026 low support treating it as a credible cycle bottom, and what would that imply for crypto exposure?**

The analysis is designed from an investment-research perspective rather than as a purely descriptive dashboard.

---

## Executive Conclusion

The research finds that the **June 30, 2026 low is a credible candidate cycle bottom, but not a confirmed cycle bottom**.

The current evidence supports a **measured exposure with confirmation** framework.

Key considerations include:

- Bitcoin recovered approximately **30.52%** from the June 30 low.
- The cycle bottom in the research model is approximately **$58.53K**.
- Peak-to-bottom drawdown was approximately **53.11%**.
- Current MVRV is approximately **1.47**, versus approximately **1.10** at the June 30 low.
- Current realized price is approximately **$53.19K**.
- Bitcoin is approximately **43.61% above realized price** in the model.
- Cumulative ETF flow in the dashboard dataset is approximately **-1.61K BTC**.
- Historical recovery behavior varies significantly across cycles, so no single recovery threshold is treated as confirmation.

The research therefore treats **$58.53K / the June 30 low as the key invalidation reference** while monitoring recovery, capital flows, valuation and derivatives positioning for confirmation.

## Investment Decision Framework

### Evidence that strengthens the bottom thesis

- Sustained recovery from the June 30 low
- Improving spot and ETF capital flows
- Stable or constructive MVRV behavior
- Controlled derivatives leverage
- Continued confirmation across independent evidence layers

### Evidence that weakens the thesis

- Sustained break below the June 30 / **$58.53K** reference
- Persistent ETF outflows alongside falling price
- Renewed severe valuation compression
- Increasing leverage combined with declining spot price
- Broader deterioration in market and on-chain conditions
